One arrival. Three accountable steps.

Every check-in moves through the same explicit path, from capture to a reportable attendance event.

  1. Capture

    Confirm a usable face

    Image-quality checks reject poor captures before recognition begins.

  2. Verify

    Test liveness and identity

    Internal ONNX models detect spoofing and compare the face embedding.

  3. Record

    Write the attendance event

    A successful check becomes an organization-scoped check-in or check-out.
